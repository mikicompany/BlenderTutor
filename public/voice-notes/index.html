AI action items

With an Anthropic API key, work mode uses Claude to turn what you say into clean action items: it fixes misheard words, splits and merges tasks, skips duplicates, and spots deadlines and urgency. Without a key, a simpler built-in method is used.

Get one at platform.claude.com. The key is stored only on this phone and sent only to Anthropic. Usage is billed to your Anthropic account.

Download Whisper once and speech-to-text runs entirely on your phone — no internet needed, and your audio never leaves the device. Words appear after you stop recording rather than live. When you're offline the app switches to it automatically.
