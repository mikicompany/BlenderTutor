Blender on the render PC

The PC downloads the official portable build from blender.org, checks its checksum and renders with it from the next job. Your installed Blender stays as it is. Updates run between jobs, never during a render.

Set up the render PC

  1. Put farm_watcher.py and start_farm.bat in the Renderfarm folder.
  2. On the PC, open start_farm.bat in Notepad and check the Blender path.
  3. Close the old watcher.bat window, then double-click start_farm.bat.
  4. Within a minute this page shows the PC as online with its GPUs.
